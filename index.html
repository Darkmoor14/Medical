OpenMed PubMed Explorer

Clinical text analysed on your device, with evidence from PubMed

Private by design. Notes are de-identified and analysed in this browser using OpenMed models. The only thing sent anywhere is the PubMed search you choose to run, and it is checked against the identifiers found in your note.
Built with OpenMed and NCBI E-utilities. For research and education. Not a medical device, and not a substitute for clinical judgement.
